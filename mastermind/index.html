Questions

Before you book.

What if my app is not done by Sunday noon?

We finish it with you on a call that week. Nobody leaves the villa without a working app on their phone, and nobody leaves the month without one on TestFlight.

Do I need a Mac or an Apple developer account?

No. Any laptop that runs Claude Code. The Sunday app is a browser app that installs to iPhone and Android. The TestFlight step runs under our account, not yours.

I did not attend the workshop. Can I come?

If Claude Code is already running on your laptop and you have built one thing with it, yes. If not, Batch 2 on Sept 19 to 20 is the door.

Do I need to be on camera?

Only as much as you want. Gwen builds the plan around your format: talking head, voiceover over b-roll, or text on screen. The photographer shoots the villa either way.

Where do I sleep?

Early bird seats get a private queen room. Regular seats share the builders' room: eight bunks and its own bath, the way a hackathon sleeps. Hosts and staff take the lanai. Most people build past midnight anyway.

I'm not techy. Will I get lost?

You won't be left alone with it. The hosts sit at the same table all night, the vault walks you through ten prompts with a video each, and the only thing you need before you arrive is Claude Code running on your laptop. If you can describe the problem, we'll help you build the fix.

What AI will I actually learn?

Three things on top of your app. Higgsfield in the content block, for AI photos and video made from your own shoot. Multi-agent AI, where several agents split one job between them, like one researching, one writing, one checking. And Hermes, below.

What is Hermes, and why learn it here?

Hermes runs AI agents on their own. You give it a job once, like checking your inbox, drafting follow-ups, or pulling a report, and it does the job on schedule and tells you what it did. ChatGPT waits for you to type. Hermes doesn't. We teach it Saturday at 7:30pm, right after dinner, then you can wire it into the app you build that night.

Will my build actually be live, or just on my laptop?

Live. By Sunday it sits on the internet at its own link, so you can open it on your phone and send it to your team or clients. It stays yours, on your own GitHub.

Refunds?

Seats are capacity, not stock. A seat can be transferred to another builder up to Oct 17. No refunds after booking.
