Questions

Before you book.

What if my app is not done by Sunday noon?

We finish it with you on a call that week. Nobody leaves the villa without a working app on their phone, and nobody leaves the month without one on TestFlight.

Do I need a Mac or an Apple developer account?

No. Any laptop that runs Claude Code. The Sunday app is a browser app that installs to iPhone and Android. The TestFlight step runs under our account, not yours.

I did not attend the workshop. Can I come?

If Claude Code is already running on your laptop and you have built one thing with it, yes. If not, Batch 2 on Sept 19 to 20 is the door.

Do I need to be on camera?

Only as much as you want. Gwen builds the plan around your format: talking head, voiceover over b-roll, or text on screen. The photographer shoots the villa either way.

Where do I sleep?

Early bird seats get a private queen room. Regular seats share the builders' room: eight bunks and its own bath, the way a hackathon sleeps. Hosts and staff take the lanai. Most people build past midnight anyway.

Refunds?

Seats are capacity, not stock. A seat can be transferred to another builder up to Oct 17. No refunds after booking.
