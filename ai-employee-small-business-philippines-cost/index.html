How Much Does an AI Employee Cost in the Philippines?

An AI employee costs two things: a one-time build and a monthly tool bill. As of November 2026, a JTL Growth build starts at USD 1,500 (PHP 75,000), with the final price quoted in writing after a call. Self-serve tools cost far less, for example Zapier from $19.99 a month, but you do the setup yourself.

The short answer on price

The price depends on who does the work. There are three routes. You can run software yourself, which costs tens of dollars a month plus your own time. You can buy an AI employee built for you, which is a one-time fee plus the tools it runs on. Or you can hire a person.

All prices below are as of November 2026. They are in USD unless marked otherwise, and each one comes from the vendor's own page. Most quotes mix up the build fee and the monthly bill. Ask for both, separately, in writing.

A build fee is not the whole cost. The tools the AI runs on still bill you each month. One outside guide says connecting an AI agent to a CRM or your own database typically starts at $500 and scales with the complexity of the integration. It adds that multi-system deployments with compliance requirements can exceed $5,000. So a build that starts at USD 1,500 sits inside a believable range for a scoped job.

A build is not the only way to buy. One Philippine provider publishes monthly plans instead. It says that for most Filipino MSMEs, AI automation costs ₱1,999 to ₱14,999 per month, and it calls ₱6,999 the sweet spot. A monthly plan is the better choice if you do not want a larger payment up front. A one-time build is the better choice if you want one specific job done and then owned. Add up twelve months before you compare the two.

Software you run yourself

If you have time and a simple task, doing it yourself is the cheapest route. Here is what the vendors list.

  • Zapier: The Free plan includes 100 tasks a month. Professional starts at $19.99 a month on annual billing for 750 tasks. Best for moving data between apps with simple rules.
  • HubSpot: The Free CRM is $0 a month for up to 2 users. Starter is $20 a month per seat at the regular price, with lower prices for new customers. Best if you mainly need a place to keep leads.
  • GoHighLevel: Agency Starter is $97 a month and includes 3 sub-accounts. Unlimited is $297 a month. Best for a coach or small agency that wants CRM, follow-up and pipeline in one tool.
  • Lindy: The Team plan is $49.99 a month for 5,000 credits. Best for building your own AI assistant without code.
  • Tidio: The Lyro AI Agent add-on starts at $32.50 a month for 50 Lyro AI conversations. Best for answering the same website chat questions all day.
  • Intercom Fin: From $0.99 per Fin outcome. Best for a busy support inbox, because you pay per result and the bill grows with volume.

These prices buy the software. They do not buy the setup, the testing or the fixing when something breaks. That is your time, or someone else's fee.

Hiring a person instead

A person is still the right choice for calls, judgment and odd cases. OnlineJobs.ph lists employer plans at $69 a month for PRO and $99 a month for Premium. Those are platform fees. The wage you pay the worker is separate, and the page does not list one.

For a point of comparison, one guide says human virtual receptionists typically start around $300 per month. An AI employee does not sleep and does not take leave. It also cannot read a tense phone call. Many small businesses end up with both: AI for the repeat work, and a person for everything else.

How to choose before the sales call

Match the route to the job. If you have one simple task and some free hours, start with a free plan from Zapier or HubSpot. If you run clients and want one system, GoHighLevel is the better fit. If chat questions eat your day, Tidio or Intercom fit better than a custom build.

If you want the job built, tested and working, with a written price, a build is worth a call. Our guide to AI employee setups compares the options by job. If your real need is a CRM, read the CRM guide for coaches and small agencies first. You may not need an AI employee yet.

Before any call, ask three things. Is this price one time or monthly? Which tools will bill me directly? Who owns the accounts when the work is done? You can also ask for a quote and compare it with the numbers above.

Questions people ask

Is there a monthly fee on top of an AI employee build?

The tools the AI runs on usually bill you monthly. A build fee does not cover that. Our public price is the build, from USD 1,500, and the final price is quoted in writing after a call. Ask any agency, us included, to list the monthly tool costs in the written quote.

Can I get an AI employee for free?

You can start free. Zapier includes 100 tasks a month on its Free plan, and the HubSpot Free CRM is $0 a month for up to 2 users. The limits are small, and you do the setup yourself. Free works for testing one idea. It rarely covers a full job.

Why is per result pricing risky?

The bill grows with volume. Intercom lists Fin from $0.99 per Fin outcome. That is fair when you get few chats and costly if a campaign sends you a flood. Ask for a monthly cap before you switch it on.

Is an AI employee cheaper than hiring a virtual assistant?

It depends on the job. A person can handle calls and judgment calls, and OnlineJobs.ph charges $69 a month for its PRO plan plus the wage you agree. An AI employee fits repeat tasks like first replies and lead follow-up. Compare the cost per task, not the headline price.

What is the cheapest way to start?

Pick one repeat task, such as replying to new leads. Try a free plan first. If you spend more than a few hours a week fixing it, a built version is worth pricing. Our <a href="/free-build/">free build page</a> explains another way to test the idea.
