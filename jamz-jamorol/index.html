02Background

He builds the systems
he sells.

Jamz Jamorol founded JTL Growth in July 2026 and runs it out of Caloocan, Metro Manila. The agency is registered in the Philippines with the Department of Trade and Industry under Business Name No. 8378934, legal name JTLGROWTH COMPUTER PROGRAMMING SERVICES.

His work sits at the point where software engineering meets operations: taking the repetitive half of a business — intake, follow-up, reporting, handoffs, content ops — and replacing it with systems that run without a person babysitting them. That means AI agents with defined jobs, CRM and workflow automation wired end to end, internal dashboards and client portals, and the websites and funnels that feed them.

He does not run the agency on one stack and sell another. JTL Growth is operated on the same multi-agent AI operations platform he builds for clients — the roster of specialised agents, the task queue, the review gates, the dashboards. Every system shipped to a client has already been run in production on his own business first.

Alongside JTL Growth he works with AVAS, the sister agency handling marketing, brand, and creative, on shared client systems and client portals.

03What he builds

Four things, done properly.

AI agents

Specialised agents with a defined job, a real trigger, and a review gate — research, copy, QA, security, reporting. Not chatbots. Workers.

Workflow & CRM automation

Lead intake, follow-up sequences, pipeline hygiene, invoicing and reporting wired end to end in GoHighLevel and custom backends.

Internal tools & portals

Client-facing portals, ops dashboards, and internal cockpits that show the real state of the business instead of a stale spreadsheet.

Websites & funnels

Custom-coded brand sites, landing pages, and conversion funnels — built to be fast, indexable, and hooked into the automation behind them.
