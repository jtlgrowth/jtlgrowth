Price guide · Updated November 2026

How much does GoHighLevel setup cost in the Philippines?

As of November 2026, the HighLevel software costs USD 97, 297 or 497 a month. A one-time setup by an agency runs from USD 499 to USD 997 for a starter build, and from USD 1,500 for white-label agency setups. Filipino freelancers bill by the hour, for example USD 15 an hour for audits. Budget for the monthly fee plus a setup fee, and get the setup price in writing.

The software fee comes first

HighLevel is the platform. You pay for it every month, no matter who builds your account. As of November 2026, the HighLevel pricing page lists three plans.

  • Starter, $97 a month. The page says "For one business or a few clients." The FAQ says it includes 3 sub-accounts.
  • Unlimited, $297 a month. The page says "For agencies managing unlimited clients."
  • Founder, $497 a month. The page describes it as perfect for founders, agencies, consultants and service providers looking to scale.

Every plan has a free 14-day trial. Billing begins automatically at the end of the trial unless you cancel. The page also warns that usage-based fees for telecommunications or third-party services may apply. Texts, calls and some add-ons are billed on top. One example: the AI Employee Growth Plan add-on is listed at $50/month per sub-account.

One business owner or coach almost always needs Starter. Unlimited and Founder make sense only if you run accounts for many clients.

What a one-time setup costs

HighLevel does not list a setup fee on its plan list. Setup fees come from third-party builders. The setup providers below publish their prices in dollars, not pesos.

AutogenCRM is a setup firm with a Wyoming address. It lists a $299 snapshot install and a $499 starter setup with a pipeline, calendar, forms and 3 workflows. Its Growth Build is $1,250 and adds a full funnel and 10 workflows. White-label agency setup is from $1,500. It says the final price is confirmed in writing after a free call.

ECOSIRE lists a $997 Setup & Onboarding package for solopreneurs and small agencies. Its White-Label SaaS package is $4,997. It says the $997 package is completed in 5-7 business days.

Where each is the better choice: the $299 install suits you if your business type is common and you mainly need your branding on a ready template. The $997 package suits you if you want a build done for your own process and some training. The white-label packages only matter if you plan to resell HighLevel.

Hiring a Filipino freelancer instead

Hourly help is the cheapest route on paper. A Philippines hiring site says the managed service 20four7VA posts advanced roles like full system audits at $15/hour. The same article says many Filipino workers offer multiple services at $10/hour. It suggests you run a $100 trial week before you commit.

For a full-time hire, one OnlineJobs.ph listing for a GoHighLevel Specialist shows a wage of PhP 30,000 for 40 hours per week.

A freelancer is the better choice when you have a clear list of tasks and time to review the work. A fixed-price agency is the better choice when you want one person answerable for the result and a written quote. You manage the freelancer. The agency manages the build.

Costs that are not in the setup quote

  • Monthly care. AutogenCRM lists Monthly Care at $297/mo for Standard or $597/mo for Pro.
  • Extra workflows. Its Custom Workflow Pack is $350 per 5 workflows.
  • Extra funnels. Funnel Design is from $499 per funnel.
  • Moving data. CRM Migration is from $799, based on record count and source platform.
  • Usage. HighLevel says usage-based fees may apply on top of any plan.

Ask every provider which of these are inside the setup price. A low setup price with paid care and paid extras can cost more by month six than a higher flat price.

Questions people ask

Does HighLevel charge a setup fee?

Its pricing page lists monthly and annual plan prices and a free 14-day trial. We saw no setup fee on that list. The setup fees in this guide come from third-party builders such as AutogenCRM and ECOSIRE.

Is there a cheaper HighLevel price for the Philippines?

Not that we could confirm. A post on the HighLevel ideas board asks the company to explore an approved $47/month entry tier. It is a request from a user, not a published plan. Check the pricing page before you rely on it.

Can I set up HighLevel myself?

Yes. There is a free 14-day trial on any plan, and Starter is $97 a month. The cost is your time. If you only need a simple pipeline and a few automations, a self-build is the cheapest route. Remember to cancel before the trial ends if you stop.

How long does a setup take?

ECOSIRE says its Setup & Onboarding package is completed in 5-7 business days. Ask any provider for a date in writing. A freelancer's timeline depends on how clear your task list is.

Will I be quoted in pesos?

The published setup prices on this page are in dollars. None of the setup providers on this page lists a peso price. Ask for the currency, the exact scope and any monthly fees in the quote before you agree.
