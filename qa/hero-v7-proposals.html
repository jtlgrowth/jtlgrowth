Five ways to close the gap between the laptop and the words.

Same laptop, same terminal, same keys and click. What changed in all five: the eyebrow is gone, the CTA no longer names a person, the keyboard deck is gone, and a jtlboard-sized mini keyboard rises inside the screen only while you type. The laptop takes the space the deck used to. Each sample tries one idea of what the white space is for. The CTA wording differs per sample on purpose: pick words and layout separately.

Every stage is live at real viewport size. Type in each one. Use the rail on the left, or press 1 to 5.

01 · Recommended

Keeby, maximised

The reference, with the wasted band closed. Laptop at 70% on the left, bezel just off the edge, copy centred in the remaining column. The mini keyboard rises inside the screen while you type and leaves when you stop.
