Five ways to put the product in the hero, a globe on panel three, and cards that move.

getkeeby.com lets you try the app before you download it. Our version: talk to the AI employee before you book the call. Every proposal below is live at real viewport size with the real copy, because the pick is about motion and a still cannot answer that. Scroll each one, drag each globe, click the chips. Use the rail on the left, or press 1 to 5.

Same three components in all five: the hero chat (scripted transcript plus click prompts, no backend), the dotted globe with pings (cobe, pings only from cities on record: Caloocan HQ, the BGC workshop venue, and you), and the eight service cards (four tiers plus four proofs, each with a one-way loop). Only the treatment changes, so the pick is a taste pick.

Pick one, or point

01 is the recommendation: it is the current jtl-mono register with the three components dropped in, so it ships fastest and nothing on the rest of the site has to change. 02 is the same build in the dark register if the globe should glow. 03 is the one to take if the globe belongs in the hero. 04 is the hardest push on "product in the hero": the chat is a console and the keys can click. 05 is the Keeby-faithful one: a play button, a sound gate, a counter, a card that follows your cursor. Pieces can be mixed: say which hero, which globe, which cards.

Not in this page: the live ping feed (needs a backend and a council), the live AI reply (needs an API, rate limits and a money grant), client names on pings (never on a public page). Plan 2 wires the pick into the home panels and the services page, then council, then push.
