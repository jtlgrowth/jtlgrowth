Five ways to open the room, none of them a dot field over the words.

The shipped hero pins two screens of the Batch 1 clip under a real time halftone at 12px cells, then sets the headline and the lead on top of it. The treatment is good. Putting the type inside it is what stings.

Every proposal below is live at real viewport size with the real clip, the real gallery and the real copy, because the complaint is about motion and a still mock cannot answer it. Scroll each one. Use the rail on the left, or press 1 to 5.

What is actually wrong

#DefectWhere it comes from
01The lattice beats against the pixel grid. A 12px dot cell at half device ratio lands near the display's own sampling frequency, so the field shimmers and re-aliases on every scroll frame instead of holding still.room-hero.js: cell 12, pr = min(dpr, 2) * 0.5
02The words sit inside the noise. Headline and lead are painted straight onto the moving dot field, so the eye refocuses continuously between type edges and dot edges..rh-copy stacked over .rh-canvas
03The lead is too faint for the ground it is on. #B9B9B9 reads fine on flat ink and stops reading on a field that is half #E2E2E2 dots..rh-copy .lead{color:#B9B9B9}
04There is no rest area in the frame. All 100svh are textured, so the eye has nowhere to park while it reads..rh-stage, full bleed canvas
01 · Recommended

Light room

kills 01 · 02 · 03 · 04

Turn the whole thing over. Paper ground, black Fraunces, and the footage lives in a contained plate below the words instead of behind them. Nothing overlays anything, so contrast is decided rather than negotiated. The one directional move: the plate widens toward edge to edge as the headline lifts away.

Workshop · Batch 1 · Aug 22 to 23, 2026 · Novel Hotel BGC

From operator to orchestrator. Shipped.

Two build days in BGC with Gwen Ayala, Faith Natividad and Jamz Jamorol. Day one every founder built an AI employee for their own business and put it live on the internet. Day two they gave it a UI system of their own and published again.

Motion

One monotonic widen, tied to scroll. No pointer effect, no ambient drift.

Contrast

#121212 on #E2E2E2 at 15.9:1 for the headline, #616161 at 4.9:1 for the lead. Both on flat paper.

Cost

Deletes room-hero.js and the three.js import from this page. Lightest of the five.

02

Quiet halftone

kills 01 · 02 · 03 · 04

Smallest change from what is live. Keep the halftone, keep the pointer lens, keep the pin. What changes: cells open from 12 to 18 and widen further toward the edges, and the centre resolves to a calm plate so the type never sits on the lattice. Same hero, without the beat frequency.

03

Split spread

kills 02 · 03 · 04

A hard seam instead of a stack. Type owns solid ink on the left, footage owns its own half on the right, and no pixel of one is ever under the other. The record reads as a fact sheet, which is what this page is. The seam slides left on scroll and the room takes the frame.

04

Contact sheet

kills 01 · 02 · 03 · 04

Drop the hero video entirely and let the 38 real frames carry the hook. They drift one way at constant speed under the headline, numbered in mono like a shoot. Hovering a frame brings it back to full colour, which is the single interaction. This is the version that proves the opening does not need footage playing behind text.

05

Card orbit

kills 01 · 02 · 03 · 04

Pick one

01 is the recommendation: it removes all four defects at once, deletes code rather than adding it, and it is the only version where the contrast under the headline cannot change no matter what the clip is doing. 02 is the safe pick if the halftone is the part worth keeping. 04 is the one to take if the hero should stop asking the browser to decode video at all.
