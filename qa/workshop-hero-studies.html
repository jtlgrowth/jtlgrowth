Four ways to close the workshop page.

The live page is one left-hand column, top to bottom: a 56-character lead under the headline, then three 62-character paragraphs, every section starting at the same left edge inside a 1240px container. The right half of the screen is bare from the fold down. Four directions below, all using the same headline, the same lead, the same agenda and the same body copy. Only the composition changes.

Every mockup is rendered at 1280×1240 in the page's real dark colours and real typefaces: the fold, then the first body section, so the rhythm below the hero is visible too. The page is theme-aware; the light register inverts the same composition.

Recommendation

A, the centred monument.

It is the composition you already picked for /services/ and the only one that removes the empty half by construction rather than by filling it. It also costs the least: the orbit, the solid chip and the fold sizing are CSS already shipped on the services page, so the port is a copy, not a build. B is the runner-up if you want the agenda above the fold; its label rail is the nicest body rhythm of the four and could be grafted onto A.
