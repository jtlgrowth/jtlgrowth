Mac app

jtlboard

Every keystroke gets a mechanical-switch sound, and a small keyboard follows your pointer and lights the key you hit.

Built on Thock by Kamil Lobinski, MIT licence. Filmed on a blank page.

jtlboard types the sentence "Every key gets a sound and a keycap." and lights each key as it lands.
