The write-ups

What each one actually does.

FWIB

A community platform for Filipina Women in Business: a directory, member profiles, and the connective tissue that lets 42 founders find and support each other. Built on GoHighLevel with a custom front end, live and growing as new members join.

What it does: hosts the member directory, onboarding, and community touchpoints in one place.

JTL Growth site

This site. Built and launched in a single night, from buying the domain to a live HTTPS deploy. The homepage runs a horizontal filmstrip; this page and the rest of the site run on the same vertical shell.

What it does: it is the agency's own storefront, services, products, workshop, and proof, all in one build.

AVAS Night Shift

A six-page brand site for AVAS, our sister agency, built without a template: custom layout, custom motion, its own visual language. It ships with a live ops dashboard behind it, not just a marketing shell.

What it does: presents the AVAS brand publicly while running real operations data underneath.

Robots & Coffee

Our digital products library: courses, workshops, and AI resources, sold direct. It's the storefront for everything we've packaged from running the agency, now hosted at ayalavirtualassistance.site.

What it does: lets people learn the system, buy the kit, and run it themselves.

AVAS AI Workshop

The event page for From Operator to Orchestrator, the two-day workshop we run in Manila with AVAS. Seat counter, early-bird countdown and checkout all on one page, so the page closes the sale instead of pointing at a form.

What it does: sells seats and holds the date, live, with no funnel builder underneath it.
