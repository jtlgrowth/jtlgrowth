Best CRM for coaches and small agencies in 2027

As of November 2026, a coach with a simple pipeline should start free. HubSpot's CRM is USD 0 for up to 2 users, and Zoho CRM is free for 3 users. Paid Zoho starts at USD 14 per user per month billed yearly. GoHighLevel starts at USD 97 a month and bundles texting and automation. Pipedrive starts at USD 14 per seat per month billed annually and suits deal-heavy agencies. We set up any of them, and you may not need us.

The short answer by type of business

No CRM is best for everyone. It depends on how many people touch leads and who keeps it clean. As of November 2026, this is how we would pick.

  • Solo coach with a few leads a week: HubSpot free CRM.
  • Small team watching cost: Zoho CRM.
  • Agency that sells proposals: Pipedrive.
  • Texting, booking and follow-up in one tool: GoHighLevel.
  • Already bought one and it sits empty: that is where we fit.

HubSpot free CRM: better for solo coaches starting out

HubSpot's pricing page shows the CRM at $0/mo: "Free for up to 2 users. No credit card required." It is the better choice for a solo coach, or a coach with one assistant, who wants a clean place to log leads on no budget.

What it needs from you each week

You do the follow-up by hand. Someone logs new leads, moves deals and clears overdue tasks. Free works when that is a short weekly pass.

Where it stops working

At the third person. The paid Starter tier shows "Starts at $7/mo/seat", but the page says discounts are for new customers only and shows "$20/mo/seat" beside it. Budget for $20. The automation most owners want sits above the free tier.

Zoho CRM: better for small teams watching cost

Zoho's pricing page says "Free forever for 3 users." Zoho's value page shows paid editions billed yearly, per user per month, in USD: Standard $14, Professional $23, Enterprise $40 and Ultimate $52. Check the billing toggle before you buy.

It is the better choice for a team of three or more, or anyone already on other Zoho apps.

What it needs from you each week

One person must own the fields, layouts and duplicate records. Zoho has many settings and nobody sets them for you.

Where it stops working

Zoho labels Standard "Beginner-friendly" and Professional "Automation and AI". For automation, plan on the $23 tier. Zoho also says local taxes (VAT, GST, etc.) are charged in addition.

Pipedrive: better for agencies that live in a deal pipeline

Pipedrive is built around deals. It is the better choice for an agency that sends proposals, tracks each one through stages and wants the team to see the pipeline at a glance.

Pipedrive's pricing page lists four plans, per seat per month, billed annually: Lite $14, Growth $24, Premium $49 and Ultimate $69, all in US dollars. Paying month to month costs more. Each plan has a free 14-day trial with no credit card.

One fact from Pipedrive's support article: "You're charged per seat, whether it's assigned to a user or not." Remove seats when someone leaves.

What it needs from you each week

Whoever sells must move deals after every call. A pipeline two weeks stale is worse than none.

Where it stops working

It is a sales tool, not a delivery tool. Booking, bulk texting and client onboarding come from other apps you connect.

GoHighLevel: better for agencies and coaches who want one tool

GoHighLevel's pricing page shows Starter at "$97 /Month", "For one business or a few clients", and the FAQ says it includes 3 Sub-Accounts. Unlimited is "$297 /Month" and Pro is "$497 /Month". The page offers a "FREE 14-day trial on any plan".

It is the better choice if you want CRM, calendar, texting, email and follow-up automation in one place, or if you run an agency and resell it.

What it needs from you each week

Someone must own it. They check that automations fire, watch the usage bill and fix broken forms.

Where it stops working

Usage. The page marks Conversation AI, Voice AI, email and SMS marketing and calling with "Usage based charges apply", and no per-unit rates appear there. A solo coach tracking a handful of leads pays for far more tool than they use.

Questions people ask

Is a free CRM enough for a coach?

Yes, if you are solo with a modest number of leads. HubSpot is free for up to 2 users and Zoho for 3. You outgrow it when you add people or want follow-up that runs without you.

Is GoHighLevel worth $97 a month?

It is if you would otherwise pay for a CRM, a texting tool, a calendar and an email tool separately. It is not if you only need a contact list. Add the usage charges before you decide.

Which CRM is cheapest for a team of three?

Zoho's free plan covers 3 users. HubSpot's free plan stops at 2. Paid HubSpot, Zoho and Pipedrive tiers charge per user, while GoHighLevel tiers are a flat monthly price.
