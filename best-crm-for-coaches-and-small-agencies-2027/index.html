Best CRM for coaches and small agencies in 2027

The best CRM for a coach or small agency is the one you will actually keep updated. Start with HubSpot's free CRM or Zoho's free edition if you want to spend nothing, Pipedrive if you sell through a pipeline, or GoHighLevel if you want follow-up and funnels in the same place.

A CRM only works if every enquiry lands in it and every next step is visible. For coaches and small agencies that means a simple pipeline, a booking link, and automatic follow-up. We compare four well-known options and explain where a build helps. Last checked: November 2026.

The options

1. HubSpot free CRM

HubSpot describes its CRM as 100% free with no expiration date. It includes contact management, deal pipelines, email templates and scheduling, a no-code form builder, a meeting scheduler that syncs with your calendar, reporting dashboards and live chat. The free tier covers up to two users and 1,000 contacts. For a solo coach it is hard to beat as a starting point. Custom reporting and some controls move to paid tiers.

2. Zoho CRM

Zoho describes its CRM as a sales CRM with built-in AI, automation, a 360 degree customer view and connections to over 1,000 apps. It offers a free trial and a freemium edition for up to three users. Its Zia assistant covers email rewriting and forecasting. It suits a small agency that wants room to grow without a large bill, though the number of settings can feel heavy at first.

3. Pipedrive

Pipedrive is built around activity-based selling: customizable pipelines, goals, task automation, email tools and a marketplace of 500+ integrations. It offers a 14-day free trial but no permanent free version. If your sales process is a clear set of stages, from enquiry to call to proposal to signed, its pipeline focus keeps you moving. It is less of a marketing tool than the others.

4. GoHighLevel

GoHighLevel combines a CRM with workflows and automations, funnels and landing pages, calendars, Missed Call Text-Back and AI tools. Its site lists plans at USD 97, 297 and 497 a month with a 14-day free trial, as of our check, and the higher plans are aimed at agencies and consultants. If you want follow-up and funnels in the same place as your contacts, it is attractive. Expect to invest time in setup, or pay someone to do it.

How to choose

Pick the cheapest CRM that fits how you sell today, not how you hope to sell next year. Solo and early: HubSpot free or Zoho free. Clear stages and a small team: Pipedrive. An agency running funnels and client work: GoHighLevel. Then decide who will connect it to your inbox and calendar, because a CRM with no follow-up behind it is a contact list.

FAQ

What is the best free CRM for a coach?

HubSpot's free CRM and Zoho's freemium edition are the two to try. HubSpot's free tier covers up to two users and 1,000 contacts. Zoho's freemium edition covers up to three users.

Do small agencies need a CRM?

Yes, once leads come from more than one place. A CRM stops enquiries living in DMs, email and memory, and shows what is stuck.

Is GoHighLevel a good CRM for a small agency?

It can be, particularly if you want funnels and follow-up in one place. It takes setup, so budget time or help to configure it.
