privacy policy

Last updated 2026

Oras collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising identifier, and no crash reporting.

Data we collect

None. The app has no server and makes no network requests. Anything you enter is stored only on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

Data we share

None, because none is collected. We do not sell, rent, or disclose personal information to anyone, for any purpose.

Third parties

Oras contains no third-party SDKs, trackers, or advertising networks.

Your data, your device

Deleting the app removes everything it stored. If you use iCloud device backup, your data may be included in that backup under Apple's terms — we have no access to it.

Purchases

Oras is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Questions: Oras support.
