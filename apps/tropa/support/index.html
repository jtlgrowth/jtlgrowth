Tropa support

Tropa is the 6-Month Side Quest Olympics for you and your friends: each one picks a sport, a craft and an ancient language, everyone posts proof for six months, and the tropa judges the finals.

Email support@jtlgrowth.com. We read everything and usually reply within two business days. Reports about a person or a post are reviewed within 24 hours.

Start a tropa

  1. Sign in with Apple and type the name your friends will see.
  2. Tap "Create a tropa", choose a name, how long it runs and the forfeit for the one who finishes last overall.
  3. Show the QR or give the 8-character code to your friends. A tropa has 3 to 8 members.
  4. Each member taps "Choose my quests" and picks three. Then log your practice, post proof at each milestone, and judge each other when the six months end.

Quick fixes

My friend cannot join

Check the 8 characters of the code, or scan the QR in the Tropa tab. A tropa holds 8 members at most. After 10 wrong codes in one hour, or 30 in one day, the app asks you to wait. A member the creator removed cannot come back with the same code.

My run did not count on the km board

The km board counts runs that Tropa recorded with GPS and runs it read from Apple Health. A log you typed by hand does not count for km, and neither does a pace faster than 2:45 per kilometer. It still counts for your week and your streak.

My activity has no map

A workout from Apple Health shows a map only when Health holds a route for it and Tropa may read routes. Open the iPhone Settings app, go to Health, Data Access and Devices, Tropa, and turn on Workout Routes. New workouts will carry the map.

The record screen says location is off

Open the iPhone Settings app, go to Tropa, Location, and choose "While Using the App". Tropa reads your location only while a recording is on.

The camera says it is off

Open the iPhone Settings app, go to Tropa, and turn on Camera. For a video, turn on Microphone too.

A friend's photo or video does not show

Pull the feed down to refresh. Photos and videos need signal the first time; after that the phone keeps them.

What I did has not reached my friends

No signal is fine. Tropa keeps what you did on the phone and sends it by itself when signal is back. Pull down to send it now.

It says "The server said no"

The server refused your last change, for example a score after the results came out. The app put things back the way the server has them. Nothing else is lost.

I started without an account and now want my friends in

Open the menu on the Home tab, tap Settings, then "Sign in with Apple". What you made without an account stays on the phone and is not uploaded.

The rules of a tropa

A tropa is a private group of friends. These rules still apply to everything posted in it:

  • No nudity or sexual content.
  • No bullying, threats or harassment, and no hate against a person or a group.
  • No one else's private information, and nothing illegal.

To report a post or an activity, open the menu on its card and tap "I-report". To report or block a member, open the Tropa tab. The creator of a tropa can remove a member there, and anyone can leave. We review every report within 24 hours, and we remove content and close accounts that break these rules.

Delete your account

  1. Open the Tropa tab and tap Account at the top right.
  2. Tap "Delete account", then confirm with Apple.

This deletes your profile, your tropa memberships and everything you posted, and it cannot be undone. If you cannot open the app, email us from the address of your Apple account and we will delete it for you.

Reporting a bug

Tell us what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. Your iPhone model and iOS version help. A screenshot helps more.
