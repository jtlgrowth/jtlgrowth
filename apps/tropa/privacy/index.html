Tropa privacy policy

Effective 14 November 2026 · JTL Growth, Philippines · support@jtlgrowth.com

The short version. Tropa is the 6-Month Side Quest Olympics for a small group of friends, a tropa of up to 8 people. What you log and post is shown to the members of your tropa and to nobody else. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we do not sell or rent your information. You can also try the app with no account, and then everything stays on your iPhone.

Two ways to use Tropa

Without an accountTap "Try it first without an account". Everything you make stays on this iPhone. Nothing is sent to us.
With Sign in with AppleYour tropa is kept on our server, so your friends see it on their own phones. The rest of this page describes this way of using the app.

What Tropa collects

Your Apple sign-inTropa uses Sign in with Apple. We receive an Apple user identifier and the email Apple shares with us, which can be Apple's private relay address if you hide yours. Other members never see your email. We do not use it to contact you unless you write to us.
Your name in the appThe name you type after you sign in. This is what your tropa sees.
Your tropaIts name, its start and end dates, the forfeit your group agreed on, its 8-character invite code, who created it and who its members are.
Your side questsThe sport, craft, ancient language and small business you chose, with your target level, the text you will read, your optional six-month goals and your weekly goal. For the business: your yes or no each month on whether it was profitable, and the Instagram, Facebook, TikTok or Shopee you add, which your tropa sees as buttons. Tropa never asks for your sales or your money.
Your activity logsThe kind of activity, when it started, how long it took, the effort you gave it, an optional title and tag, and the distance for a run, walk, hike or ride. Members can like and comment on a log.
Location, only while you recordWhen you record a run, walk or ride with Tropa, the app reads your location until you stop the recording, also while the screen is locked. It saves the route line, the distance and the name of the town. By default the first and last part of the route are cut before it is saved, so the place where you start and finish does not show. Tropa never reads your location when no recording is on.
Apple Health, if you turn it onTropa reads your running, walking, hiking and cycling workouts from Apple Health: the start time, the duration, the distance and the route when there is one. They become activity logs in your tropa, with the start and end of each route always cut. Tropa also reads your heart rate during those workouts to show you your own heart rate and zones. Heart rate stays on your phone: it is read when you open a workout, never saved by Tropa, never sent to our servers and never shown to your tropa. Tropa also reads your daily step count from Apple Health to show your steps on Home. Steps are read on your phone and stay there unless you turn on "Share with the tropa" on the Steps card. Sharing is off until you turn it on, and you turn it on for one tropa at a time. Your yes is kept on our servers, and without it the server takes no steps from any phone. While sharing is on, Tropa sends one number for each day, your step total for that day, so the members of that tropa see it on the Steps board. Today's total is updated during the day, at most once an hour. Nothing else from Apple Health is sent. If you block a member, or a member blocks you, neither of you sees the other's steps. When you turn sharing off, your yes and the days you shared with that tropa are deleted from our servers. The max heart rate you can type in Settings stays on your phone too. Tropa never writes to Apple Health. The workouts, and your day totals if you share them, are shown to your tropa only. Nothing from Apple Health is ever used for advertising or marketing, and it is never sold or given to anyone else.
Posts, photos and videosThe photo or video you take or pick, your caption, whether a business post asks for support, and the reactions, "seen" marks and Support taps from members. Tropa uses the camera and the microphone only when you take a photo or video or scan an invite QR.
NudgesWhen you nudge a member, we keep who nudged whom and when.
Your judging scoresThe scores you give each member's quests at the end of the cycle. They stay hidden from other members until the tropa reveals the results. After the reveal the scores are sent to the members' phones to show the results.
Reports and blocksIf you report a person, a post or a log, we keep the report, your reason and a copy of what you reported (without any route). If you block a member, we keep that so their posts and logs stay hidden from you.
Failed join attemptsWhen you type a wrong invite code, we note the time so codes cannot be guessed. We delete these notes within 3 days.
How much you saveFor each day we count how many times you saved something and how much data that added. The count holds none of your content. It keeps one account from filling the service, and it is dropped after 30 days. Photos and videos have a limit per member too.

Where it is kept

Your tropa's records, photos and videos are stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, on servers in Singapore, in a private database and private file storage. They travel over encrypted connections.

Your iPhone keeps a copy so the app works without signal, and it downloads your tropa's photos and videos to show them in the feed. When you sign out, the app removes that copy and those files from the phone.

Who can see it

The current members of your tropa, and nobody else. Someone who leaves or is removed can no longer read the tropa. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it with advertisers. Our team can reach the stored data only to run Tropa, to act on a report, or to answer a request from you.

Third parties

  • Supabase hosts the database and the files. The Supabase library in the app talks only to our Tropa server.
  • Cloudflare stores an encrypted copy of our database backups. Only we hold the key.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple. Apple Maps draws the map under a route and gives the name of the town, so the points of that route are sent to Apple for that purpose.

The app has no advertising code, no analytics and no crash reporting service.

How long it is kept, and deleting it

One post or logOpen the menu on the card and tap "Delete". It is removed from the server and from the other members' phones at their next refresh.
Leaving a tropaIn the Tropa tab, tap "Leave the tropa". Your posts, logs, side quests, likes, comments and shared step totals in that tropa are deleted from the server. Your photos and videos there are removed from storage within 7 days. The same happens when the creator removes you.
Deleting your accountIn the Tropa tab, tap Account, then "Delete account". The same button is on the first screen after you sign in. This deletes your profile, every tropa membership, your posts, logs, photos, videos, shared step totals, judging scores, likes, comments and blocks, and it revokes the app's Sign in with Apple access. It cannot be undone.
Shared step totalsOn the Steps card, turn off "Share with the tropa". Your yes and the days you shared with that tropa are deleted from the server. A shared day is shown for 60 days at most and is then deleted from the server, also when you never open the app again.
What staysA report you sent stays for our review, without your identity. Reports about you are deleted with your account, and your comments are taken out of the copies saved with other reports. When the last member leaves a tropa, the tropa is deleted.
BackupsWe back up the database every day so a failure does not lose your tropa. A backup holds no sign-in session and no photo or video. What you delete stays inside older backups until they age out, 45 days at most, and is used for nothing else. If we ever restore a backup, accounts deleted since then are deleted again.

Until you delete them, your records are kept while your account exists, except shared step totals, which are deleted after 60 days.

Children

Tropa is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)

You may ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to how we use it. Most of this you can do in the app. For anything else write to support@jtlgrowth.com. You may also contact the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

The App Store

Apple's App Store handles the download under its own privacy policy. Tropa is free, with no purchases and no ads. We receive only the standard, anonymous download reports Apple gives every developer.

Changes

If Tropa starts to collect anything new, we will change this page first and tell you in the app before it happens.
