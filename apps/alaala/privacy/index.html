Alaala privacy policy

Effective 9 November 2026 · JTL Growth, Philippines · support@jtlgrowth.com

The short version. Alaala keeps your journal on your iPhone only. It is free, with no account, no server, no ads and no tracking. We never see, receive or hold your notes, photos or voice recordings, and the app works with no internet at all.

What the app stores, and where

Your notesThe text, the time and the notebook they belong to. Kept in the app's storage (SwiftData) on this iPhone.
Your photosPhotos you pick from your library or take with the camera. Alaala keeps a copy inside the app; the original stays in Photos.
Your voice notesRecordings you make in Alaala, and the text of what was said, which you can edit. Kept on this iPhone.
Your settingsFor example your chosen transcription language. Kept on this iPhone.

Nothing else: no contacts, no location, no usage data. Alaala has no analytics, no crash reporting service and no advertising code.

Voice notes and transcription

The microphone turns on only when you tap record. To turn a recording into text, Alaala uses Apple's Speech framework, and the transcription happens on your iPhone: the audio does not leave it.

There is one optional setting, "Allow Apple's online transcription". It is off by default, and you can turn it on or off again in the record screen. Only if you turn it on, and only when on-device transcription is not available for your language, the recording is sent to Apple's speech service to be converted to text. That audio goes to Apple, never to us, and Apple handles it under its own privacy policy.

Photos and camera

The camera opens only when you add a photo with it. The photo picker shows only what you choose. Alaala keeps a copy of the photos you choose inside the app.

Siri and Shortcuts

Alaala offers shortcuts to count your notes, add a text note and open the app. They run on your iPhone and only when it is unlocked. Alaala sends nothing to us.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, email or phone number.
  • No server: Alaala makes no network connections of its own.
  • No advertising, no ad IDs, no analytics or tracking.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with anyone, because we never have it.

Backups

Your journal is part of the normal app data on your iPhone, so Apple's iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone can include it, under your own Apple settings. We do not run a backup or sync of our own.

Deleting your data

Delete a note from its editor, or a notebook from the shelf; its photos and recordings are deleted with it. Deleting the Alaala app removes everything it stored on the iPhone.

Children

Alaala is a general journal and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)

We hold nothing about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or erase: edit or delete anything inside the app. Questions or concerns: support@jtlgrowth.com. You may also contact the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

The App Store

Apple's App Store handles the download under its own privacy policy. Alaala is free and has no purchases. We receive only the standard, anonymous download reports Apple gives every developer.

Changes

If a future version ever needs anything to leave your phone (for example optional cloud storage), we will update this policy first and ask inside the app before it happens.
