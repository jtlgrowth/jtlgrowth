Pitaka support

Pitaka: ID Wallet keeps your IDs on your iPhone and fills them into forms.

Email support@jtlgrowth.com. We read everything and usually reply within two business days. Please never send us an ID number or a photo of an ID.

Turn on AutoFill (one time)

  1. Open the iPhone Settings app.
  2. Go to General, then AutoFill & Passwords.
  3. Turn on Pitaka.

Pitaka shows these steps too: tap the ••• menu on the home screen, then "Turn on AutoFill".

Fill a form

  1. Tap a field in any app or website.
  2. Tap the field again (or long-press it), then choose AutoFill, then Pitaka.
  3. Pass Face ID, then tap the value you want. It goes into the field.

To practise first, open Pitaka, tap the ••• menu, then "Try it". It opens a sample form inside the app.

Quick fixes

Pitaka is not in the AutoFill menu

Check Settings, General, AutoFill & Passwords: Pitaka must be on. Pitaka needs iOS 18 or later.

The camera says it is off

Open the iPhone Settings app, go to Pitaka, and turn on Camera. You can also type the ID by hand, or pick a photo instead.

The scan read a field wrong

Fix it on the review screen before you save, or open the ID later and tap edit. Good light and a flat ID help.

Face ID failed

Try again, or use your iPhone passcode. Pitaka never stores Face ID data; iOS handles it.

New phone, and my IDs are gone

That is by design. Your IDs never leave the old phone: no iCloud, no backup, no server. Scan or type them again on the new phone.

Delete everything

Tap the ••• menu, then "Where are my IDs kept?", then Delete all. Do this before you delete the app.

Reporting a bug

Tell us what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. Your iPhone model and iOS version help. A screenshot helps more, but cover any ID details first.
