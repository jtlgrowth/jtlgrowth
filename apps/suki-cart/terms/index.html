1. What Suki Cart is

Suki Cart is a shopping calculator. You type the prices you see, and it keeps a running total against a budget you set, including per-kilo items, promos such as Buy 1 Take 1 and bundles, and an estimate of senior citizen and PWD discounts.

2. Totals are estimates

Every total is based on the prices and quantities you enter. The store's receipt is always final: shelf prices, promos, rounding and what a store treats as eligible can differ at the counter. We are not responsible for a difference between the app's estimate and what a store charges.

3. Senior citizen and PWD discounts

The app estimates the discounts in Philippine law: 20% off and VAT exemption on medicines and other covered goods (RA 9994, RA 10754), and 5% off basic necessities and prime commodities, VAT included, up to ₱125 a week on ₱2,500 of purchases (DTI-DA-DOE Joint Administrative Order 24-02, 2024). The store decides what is eligible and needs a valid Senior Citizen or PWD ID. Laws and orders change; the estimate may lag a change.
