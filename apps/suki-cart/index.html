Suki Cart help

Suki Cart is a grocery cart calculator for Filipino shoppers. Type each price, see what is left of your budget, and let Suki fix the cart when you go over. In Filipino or English.

Need help or found a bug? Email jamzjamorol03@gmail.com. Say what phone you use and what you tapped.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in, email or phone number. Your trips stay on your phone.

How do I switch between Filipino and English?

On the start screen, tap the Fil or EN chip at the top, next to Sound and Look. Every screen changes at once.

What is free, and what does Suki Pro add?

Free for life: the cart and budget, Senior/PWD discount math, B1T1 and bundles, the shopping list, the budget until payday, and Bahay (Family). Suki Pro (₱149 once, no subscription) adds price-tag scanning, Suki's smart fix that swaps for a cheaper match, and price history. Your first 3 trips have Pro for free.

I changed phones. How do I get Suki Pro back?

Open Suki Pro from the start screen and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

How do I move my trips to a new phone?

Suki Cart is part of your phone's own backup. To move by hand: Backup, then Save to Files (or Send), and on the new phone Backup, then Open a backup file.

How does Bahay (Family) work?

One person taps Bahay (Family), then Make a Family, and shares the 6-character code. Others type the code under Have a code? Each person shops on their own phone; finished trips, the shared list and the payday budget show for everyone. Leaving deletes your trips from the server.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app to erase everything on your phone. If you are in a Bahay, first open Bahay (Family) and tap Leave the Family: that erases your trips from our server. Details are in the privacy policy.

Is the total exactly what the cashier will charge?

It is an estimate from the prices you type. The store receipt is final: shelf prices, promos and rounding can differ at the counter.
