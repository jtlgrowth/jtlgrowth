Privacy policy

Last updated 29 September 2026

Hugas keeps a household's dish-washing turns and the photo that proves each one. Only the people in your household can see them. There are no analytics, no advertising identifier and no crash reporting in the app.

What Hugas collects

  • Your household membership: your display name, which household you belong to, and your place in its turn order.
  • Turn records: who completed each turn and when.
  • Proof photos: the photo you take of the clean sink when you finish your turn. Hugas uses the camera only when you tap to take that photo, and it never reads your photo library.
  • Sign-in details: what is needed to keep you signed in to your household account.

Where it is kept

Turn records and photos are stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, in private storage. They travel over encrypted connections. A photo is opened in the app through a link that expires after 5 minutes.

On your phone, a photo stays only until the server has it, so a proof taken without signal is not lost. Once the turn is saved, the phone's copy is deleted. While it waits, the photo is protected by iOS file encryption.

Who can see it

The members of your household, and nobody else. We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone for advertising or any other purpose. Our team can reach the stored data only to operate Hugas or to answer a request from you.

Third parties

The app includes one third-party library, the Supabase client, which talks only to our Hugas database and storage. There are no ad networks or trackers in the current app. If a free version with ads ships, this page will name the ad network and what it receives before that version is released.

Purchases

Any purchase in Hugas is handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

How long it is kept, and deleting it

Turn records and photos are kept while your household uses Hugas. To delete your data or your whole household's, email support@jtlgrowth.com from the address you use with us, and we will delete it within 30 days and confirm when it is done. Deleting the app removes anything still waiting on your phone.

Children

Hugas is made for households and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13 without a parent or guardian. If you believe a child's information is in Hugas without that consent, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

When this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
