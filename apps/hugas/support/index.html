Quick fixes

The camera says it is off

Open the iPhone Settings app, go to Hugas, and turn on Camera. Then come back to Hugas; it picks up the change on its own.

My proof is still waiting

No signal is fine. The photo is saved on your phone, and Hugas sends it by itself once signal is back, as long as the app is open or the next time you open it. Then the turn moves on.

It says someone finished first

Another member logged that turn before your photo arrived, so the turn had already moved on. Nothing is broken. Check the pila to see who is next.

The wrong person is signed in

Go to the Puso tab and tap "Palitan kung sino ka".

Text is cut off

Hugas is built to work at every text size. Tell us which screen and your text size setting, and we will fix it.

Reporting a bug

Tell us what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. Your iPhone model and iOS version help. A screenshot helps more.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds directly, but tell us what went wrong so we can fix it.
