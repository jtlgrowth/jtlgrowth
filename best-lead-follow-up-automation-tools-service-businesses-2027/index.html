Best lead follow-up automation tools for service businesses in 2027

The best lead follow-up tool is the one that replies to every new enquiry within minutes, tells you it happened, and gets the lead to a booking link. For a field service business that is usually an all-in-one like Jobber or GoHighLevel. For a custom process, it is an automation tool or a build.

Service businesses lose jobs in the gap between an enquiry arriving and a human replying. Follow-up automation closes that gap. We compare tools on three things: instant reply, a nudge if the lead goes quiet, and a clear path to a booking. Last checked: November 2026.

The options

1. Jobber

Jobber is made for service businesses. Its site describes covering a job from first quote to final payment, with scheduling, invoicing and payments, and says it can turn leads into customers with fast follow-ups and automated reminders. If you run a home service or field team and want one system for quotes, jobs and getting paid, it is a natural fit. If your sales process is unusual, expect to work within its structure.

2. GoHighLevel

GoHighLevel lists a CRM, workflows and automations, sales pipelines, appointment reminders, funnels and landing pages, and Missed Call Text-Back, plus Voice AI and Conversation AI. Its site lists plans at USD 97, 297 and 497 a month, each with a 14-day free trial, as of our check. It is aimed at agencies and growing businesses, and it can do a lot. That breadth is also the catch: it needs to be set up properly, and many owners never finish.

3. HubSpot free CRM

HubSpot describes its CRM as free with no expiration date. It includes contacts, deal pipelines, email templates and scheduling, a no-code form builder, a meeting scheduler and live chat, for up to two users and 1,000 contacts. For a small team that wants a tidy lead list and a booking link without paying, it is a good starting point. Deeper automation sits in paid tiers.

4. Zapier and Make

If you already have a form, a calendar and a CRM, the missing piece is the glue. Zapier connects 9,000+ apps and has a free plan with 100 tasks a month. Make lists 3,500+ apps and a free plan with no time limit. A typical flow is new form entry, instant text or email, alert to the owner, and a reminder if there is no booking. These tools give you flexibility, and in return you own the testing and the upkeep.

5. Lindy

Lindy describes an AI employee that gives every new lead a fast, personal reply even after hours, answers calls and books time on your calendar, and follows up on open proposals. It says anything with outside impact waits for your approval. It is worth a look if you want a ready-made assistant rather than a workflow you design.

How to choose

If you run jobs and invoices, start with Jobber. If you want one tool for marketing and follow-up and can invest in setup, look at GoHighLevel. If you have almost no budget, use HubSpot free and a calendar link. If you already have the tools and just need them connected, use Zapier or Make. If you want it done and watched for you, book a build. Whatever you pick, test it with a real enquiry before you trust it.

FAQ

How fast should lead follow-up be?

As fast as you can make it. An instant acknowledgement, even a short one, is better than a perfect reply the next day. Automation lets you do the first part at any hour.

What is missed-call text back?

An automatic text sent when you miss a call, so the caller gets a reply instead of silence. GoHighLevel lists it as a feature, and it can also be built with automation tools.

Do I need a CRM for lead follow-up?

You need somewhere to record each lead and what happened. It can be a free CRM such as HubSpot's, or the CRM inside a tool like Jobber or GoHighLevel.

Should I use automation tools or hire someone to build it?

If you have the hours and enjoy it, build it. If you would rather spend them on jobs, have it built. The risk in DIY is the testing and the upkeep, not the first version.
