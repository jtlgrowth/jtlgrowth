Best lead follow-up automation tools for service businesses in 2027

Start with what you already use

Lead follow-up is a plain job. An enquiry comes in. Someone replies fast, then chases a few more times. Every tool below can do some version of that. What differs is who keeps it running and where it breaks. Prices are from each vendor's own pricing page, read in November 2026.

Jobber

Jobber is field service software for quotes, scheduling and invoices. Core starts at $29/mo for one user, the lowest of the three prices the page shows for that plan. Connect, which the page pitches at pros who want to automate tasks, shows $99/mo for one user at its lowest. Extra users cost $29/mo each.

Each week it needs you to keep client records and job status honest. Open quotes that nobody chased are the usual leak. It stops working when your leads come from ads, DMs and web forms and you run no visits or crews. It is a job tool first, not a lead inbox. Better choice for: lawn, cleaning, HVAC and repair businesses with scheduled visits.

GoHighLevel

GoHighLevel is the all-in-one option: pipelines, texts, email, calendars and workflows in one login. The Starter plan is $97 /Month. The page says usage based charges apply, so your real bill is the plan plus usage for email, SMS, calling and AI.

Someone has to build the workflows, set up phone numbers and read the failures each week. It stops working when nobody on your side wants to learn it. You then pay for software that sits half set up. Better choice for: agencies and owners who will spend real time inside one platform. Our CRM comparison for coaches and small agencies goes deeper.

HubSpot free CRM

HubSpot lists its free CRM at $0/mo, free for up to 2 users with no credit card required. Starter is normally priced at $20/month per seat. The weekly job is manual: log new leads, move deals along and send your own follow-ups. Check which automatic follow-up features sit in the free tier before you rely on it. That is where free plans usually stop. Better choice for: a solo owner or coach with a modest lead flow who mainly needs a clean list. If that is you, you do not need to pay anyone yet.

Automation glue and AI agents

Zapier and Make

These tools connect apps you already own. A form fills, a row is added, a text goes out. Zapier's Free plan gives 100 tasks/mo. Pro at 750 tasks is $19.99 a month on annual billing. Make's Free plan gives 1000 credits per month. Make's paid plans at 10,000 credits a month are $9 for Core, $16 for Pro and $29 for Teams, billed annually.

Every step counts. A five-step follow-up for 150 leads uses 750 tasks. Each week, check the failed runs. When an app changes a field or a login expires, a zap fails and no lead hears from you. They also do not read replies or judge tone. Better choice for: an owner who already has a form, a spreadsheet and a texting tool and can click through a flow builder.

Lindy

Lindy builds AI agents that read and answer messages. The Free plan gives $50 in credits that last 7 days. Team is $49.99/mo for 5,000 credits. Top-ups cost $10 per 1,000 credits.

The weekly job is reading what the agent actually sent and fixing wrong answers. Cost rises with message volume, because you pay in credits. It stops working when you let it handle pricing or booking decisions without a check. Better choice for: an owner who wants an AI to draft and send replies and is happy to test and tune it.

Questions people ask

What is the cheapest way to follow up leads automatically?

HubSpot's free CRM costs $0/mo for up to 2 users, and Zapier's Free plan gives 100 tasks a month. Both cost no cash. They cost your time, and the free tiers cap what you can automate.

Is GoHighLevel really $97 a month?

The Starter plan is listed at $97 /Month. The page also says usage based charges apply. Budget for the plan plus those charges, and ask who will build and maintain the workflows.

Do I need a CRM, or is Zapier enough?

If you only need a form to trigger a text, Zapier can be enough. If you want to see every lead, its stage and who last replied, you need a CRM. HubSpot's free tier covers that for 2 users.

Will an AI agent like Lindy answer leads without my help?

It can send replies on its own, but you pay in credits. Read its sent messages every week, and do not let it quote prices or promise slots until you have tested it.
