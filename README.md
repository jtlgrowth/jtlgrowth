### We run the firm on the thing we sell

Two people and an AI workforce. Fifteen-plus AI seats work the repetitive half of the

most of the shop is software we built and maintain. The repos below are pieces of it.

### What we build

Four tiers, installed in order, because each only works when the one below it does.
**1. Funnels and websites**, hand-coded, tracking and consent wired at build time ·
**2. Automation systems**, CRM, routing, follow-up, with retries, alerts and a human
fallback designed in · **3. AI employees**, agents that answer, qualify and follow up in
inbox, chat and CRM, disclosing that they are automated · **4. Ops portals**, people, work
in flight and revenue on one screen.

Built on Claude, GPT-5, n8n, GoHighLevel, Supabase, FastAPI and Playwright.
No template, no page builder, no drag-and-drop.
