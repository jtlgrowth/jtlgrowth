Before you start

Questions, answered.

The short answers. Not on the list? Ask it in the last row.

Where do I start?

With tier one, your site and funnel. We install four layers in order, and each one pays for the next. You do not buy tier three before tier one works.

How much does a build cost?

One public from-price: the AI employee, shown on its page. Every other build is scoped and quoted in writing, against what is already wired in your business. You get a count you can check and a sentence saying where it stops. Request a quote.

Will I be locked into a page builder?

No. Landing pages and full sites are hand-coded, so there is no builder lock-in.

Is an AI employee just a chatbot?

No. Each one is an agent with one job, a written job description and real tools, trained on your process, your voice and your edge cases. Its automated replies say they are automated, by policy.

What happens when an automation fails?

Failure paths are designed in from the start: retries, alerts, and a human fallback.

Which tools do you work with?

CRM and pipelines on GoHighLevel or a custom backend, plus the tool-to-tool plumbing that stops your team retyping data.

What if my business does not need AI?

Then we tell you that on the call.

Where are you based?

Metro Manila, Philippines, serving clients in the PH, the US and the EU.
