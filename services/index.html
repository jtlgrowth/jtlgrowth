The ladder

Each tier only works
because the one under it does.

A funnel with no automation leaks. Automation with no AI seat still needs a human in the loop at 2am. An AI seat with no portal is invisible to your team. The summit (a business that runs itself) stays locked, on purpose. We build to four, not to a fantasy.

Built by hand on Claude, GPT‑5, n8n, GoHighLevel, Supabase, FastAPI and Playwright. No template, no page builder, no drag-and-drop.

Tier 1

Funnels & Websites

Pages engineered around one question: does the visitor take the next step? Copy, flow and tracking included, not bolted on after launch.

  • 01Landing pages and full sites, hand-coded, no builder lock-in.
  • 02Copy written against the offer, not around it.
  • 03Analytics and conversion events wired at build time, behind consent.
  • 04Speed, accessibility and search visibility treated as requirements.

Tier 2

Automation Systems

Your tools, wired together. A lead triggers the sequence, the booking confirms itself, nothing slips, while you sleep.

  • 01CRM and pipeline automation on GoHighLevel or a custom backend.
  • 02Lead capture, routing, follow-up and reminder sequences.
  • 03Tool-to-tool plumbing so data stops being retyped.
  • 04Failure paths designed in: retries, alerts, and a human fallback.

Tier 3

AI Employees

Trained agents that answer, qualify and follow up inside your inbox, chat and CRM. Around the clock, on your process, not a generic chatbot.

  • 01An agent per job, with a written job description and real tools.
  • 02Trained on your process, your voice and your edge cases.
  • 03Automated replies that disclose they are automated, by policy.
  • 04A review loop, so the agent gets corrected instead of trusted blindly.

Tier 4

Ops Portals

Your team, tasks and pipeline on one screen. AI seats handle the intake and the reporting; the humans handle the calls.

  • 01One dashboard for people, work in flight and revenue.
  • 02Agent seats reporting into the same board your team reads.
  • 03Client-facing portals where that is the product.
  • 04Built on the same stack we run our own operations on daily.
