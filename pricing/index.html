Terms and conditions, in plain sentences

Where every number stops.

Scope creep is not a personality problem, it is a definition problem. Here are the definitions, in the same words that appear in the contract.

We do not sell unlimited

We sell a number of requests in progress at a time with an uncapped queue behind it, which is the same promise written honestly. A vendor who sells unlimited and does not publish a concurrency limit is selling you their queue, not their capacity.

Revisions

Two rounds per asset. A round is one consolidated set of comments from you, delivered together. Comments that arrive after we have started the next round begin a third round, quoted at $175 an hour with a 15 minute minimum.

Support hours

9am to 6pm Philippine time, Monday to Friday, excluding Philippine public holidays. Turnaround clocks run on those hours only. A request submitted at 5:50pm Friday starts its clock at 9am Monday. We do not run an out of hours rota and we will not sell you a clock we cannot staff.

If we are waiting on you

Delivery dates assume we have your assets, access and approvals. If we are waiting more than 5 business days on you or on a third party vendor, the delivery date moves by the length of the wait. The price does not move.

Scope freeze

Scope freezes at the end of the first milestone. Everything after that is a change order, quoted in writing and approved by you before any work starts. We would rather have an awkward conversation in week two than send you an invoice you did not expect in week six.

Pass-through costs

Platform fees, phone registration and campaign fees, SMS and voice usage, model API spend, hosting, domains and stock licences are passed to you at cost with the vendor invoice attached to ours. We never mark them up and we never absorb them, so you can always check.

What is never included

Advertising spend and paid media management, video shoots, actors and locations, 3D, print production, long form document design, and any platform we do not hold a licence for. If you need one of those we will name someone good and take nothing for the introduction.

How the work is made

Parts of this work are produced with AI systems we build and run ourselves. That is how this firm works and it is why the turnaround is what it is. Every deliverable is reviewed and signed by a named person before it reaches you, and that person is named in your contract.

Terms

The rest of the answers.

Minimum term
Three months, then month to month. Six months waives the setup fee. Twelve months takes 12 percent off the monthly and locks your price against any increase.
What happens on a call
A 20 minute fit call, free, to work out whether the diagnostic is the right purchase. Nothing is scoped on it and nothing is quoted on it. That is what the diagnostic is for.
If the answer is no
A diagnostic can end in a written do not build. The fee is not refunded, and that sentence is on the order form above the pay button. We would rather charge you $1,500 to stop than $9,500 to start something that will not work.
Payment
Bank transfer or card. Invoiced monthly in advance. Annual and quarterly options are on the tier, not a negotiation.
Who does the work
A named operator, in Manila, who signs your deliverables and appears on your calls. Not a rotating pool, and not a ticket queue with a first name attached.
Larger builds
Multi system and multi department work is scoped from the diagnostic and quoted as a fixed price with milestones. There is no number for it on this page because there is no honest number until we have looked.
