Best AI employee setups for small businesses in 2027

The best AI employee setup for a small business is one AI doing one job, wired into the tools you already use and checked every week. If you have the time, start with a no-code builder. If you do not, have one built for you.

An AI employee is not a chat window. It has a job (answer new leads, book calls, handle first-line support), it is connected to your inbox, calendar or CRM, and someone reads what it did. That is the standard we use below. Last checked: November 2026.

The options

1. A chat assistant with your own instructions

This is where almost everyone starts. You write down the job, paste in your offer and your usual answers, and use the assistant to draft replies. It costs very little and it teaches you what the job really involves. The limit is that nothing is connected. Every reply is copied somewhere by hand, which is why most people stop after a few weeks. Treat it as a rehearsal for a real setup, not the setup itself.

2. Zapier

Zapier describes itself as the control plane that connects any AI to any tool, with access to 9,000+ apps and AI agents that take action across your apps. It has a free plan with 100 tasks a month. It is a strong choice if your tools are scattered across many apps and you are comfortable building and debugging workflows yourself. The cost is your time: someone has to build it, test it with messy real inputs, and notice when it breaks.

3. Make

Make calls itself an AI automation platform where you design workflows and agents on a visual canvas, by prompting or drag and drop. It lists 3,500+ apps and a free plan with no time limit. The visual canvas suits people who like to see every step. Like Zapier, it is a toolkit, not a finished employee. You decide the job, wire it, and keep it running.

4. Lindy

Lindy calls its product an AI employee for business work. Its site describes replying to every new lead quickly, even after hours, answering calls and booking time on your calendar, following up on open proposals, and preparing you for meetings. It also says anything with outside impact waits for your approval, which is a sensible default. It is the closest to a ready-made option. Check that your process fits how it works before you commit.

5. Fin by Intercom

Fin is an AI agent for customer service. Its site says it resolves questions across channels such as chat, email, voice and social, takes actions in connected systems, and hands over to your team with context. It works natively with Intercom and with other helpdesks including Zendesk, HubSpot and Freshdesk. Pricing is described as outcome based. If your main pain is a growing support inbox, this is built for exactly that. If your pain is slow follow-up on new leads, it is the wrong tool.

How to choose

Ask three questions. Is the job clear enough to write in one paragraph? If not, start with the chat assistant and learn. Do you have the hours to build, test and maintain it? If yes, Zapier or Make will serve you well. If you need it working in weeks and watched by someone else, a build or a ready-made tool is the better use of your time. Whichever you pick, give the AI one job first. Two jobs is how projects stall.

FAQ

What is an AI employee?

An AI system with one defined job, such as replying to new leads or booking calls, connected to your real tools and reviewed on a schedule. A chat window you type into is an assistant, not an employee.

How much does an AI employee cost?

It depends on the route. Chat assistants and the free plans of Zapier and Make cost little but cost your time. A JTL Growth AI employee build starts at USD 1,500, with the final price quoted in writing after a call.

Can I build one myself?

Yes. Write the job down, draft it in a chat assistant, then connect it with Zapier or Make. The parts that usually fail are wiring it to your CRM and calendar, and checking it after launch.

What should my first AI employee do?

The job that breaks first when you get twice as busy. For most small businesses that is the first reply to a new lead, because slow replies lose the enquiry.

Do I need a CRM first?

Not always, but it helps. An AI employee needs somewhere to read and write lead details. If you have no CRM, a free one is enough to start.
