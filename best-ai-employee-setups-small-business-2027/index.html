What each option asks of you, and where it stops

Chat assistant (ChatGPT or Claude)

You are the manager and the hands. Claude Pro is $17 a month billed annually, or $20 billed monthly. ChatGPT Plus is $20 a month, billed monthly. Your instructions live in a saved project or custom GPT. Each week you paste in the work, read the output and fix the instructions when it drifts. It stops where you stop: it does not watch your inbox or act on its own.

Zapier

Zapier runs fixed steps: when this happens, do that. The free plan includes 100 tasks a month. Pro at 750 tasks is $19.99 a month billed annually. Each week, open the run history and fix a changed login or form field. The usual failure is a flow that breaks quietly and nobody notices for days. It stops at judgment: a messy reply still needs a person to decide what to do.

Make

Make bills in credits, and each action uses some. The free plan gives you 1000 credits a month. Paid plans at 10,000 credits a month are $9 for Core, $16 for Pro and $29 for Teams, billed annually. Each week, check failed scenarios and credit use. It suits owners who like drawing branches on a canvas. It stops at rules, not judgment, and a big scenario is hard for anyone but its author to repair.

Lindy

Lindy is the closest to a ready-made assistant that does tasks for you. The free plan gives $50 in credits, but they last 7 days. The Team plan is priced by credits: $49.99 a month buys 5,000. Each week, review what it did and tighten its instructions. Usage drives the bill, so run it for a month before you trust a budget.

Fin by Intercom

Fin answers customer questions from your help content at $0.99 per outcome. You also need an Intercom seat, and Essential is $19 per seat a month for new customers, billed annually. Each week, read the chats it could not resolve and write the missing answers into your help articles. It stops at support. It will not run your sales follow-up, and the bill grows with volume.

Questions people ask

Is there one best AI employee setup for a small business?

No. A chat assistant fits writing and planning, Zapier or Make fit moving data between apps, Fin fits support, and a custom build fits a routine you want handled end to end.

Is ChatGPT or Claude enough on its own?

For drafting, research and planning, yes. Claude Pro is $17 a month billed annually, and ChatGPT Plus is $20 a month. It will not watch your inbox or run steps on its own, so you still do the hands-on work.

Should I choose Zapier or Make?

They count usage differently. Zapier's free plan includes 100 tasks a month. Make's free plan gives 1000 credits a month. Those are not the same unit, so test your real flow on each free plan before you pay.

What does Fin really cost?

Fin is $0.99 per outcome. You also need an Intercom seat, and Essential is $19 per seat a month for new customers, billed annually. Multiply your monthly outcomes by the rate before you commit.
