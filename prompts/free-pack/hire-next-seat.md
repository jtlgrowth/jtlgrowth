# Hire your next seat

Walks the public hire skill to add one more AI employee, picking the role from what the business does.

## The prompt

Paste this into Claude Code.

```
I already have one AI employee hired with the hire skill. I want to hire my next one.

Use the hire skill (it is already installed) to walk me through this. Before you start the skill's own questions, ask me: what is the one task in my business that eats the most time right now, the one I would hand off first if I could.

From my answer, recommend the type of employee that fits best (someone who finds things, sorts things, writes things, answers things, or watches for things), tell me why, and let me agree or pick a different one.

Then run the hire skill for real, in team mode, and actually write the files, do not just describe what it would write. Ask its questions in the same one-thing-at-a-time way, and when I say "you pick" or "I don't know," take the recommended option and tell me which one you took.

When the hire is done, tell me the new employee's name, what it owns, and the exact first task I should hand it today.

To check it worked: ask the new employee (by name) to do that first real task, and see whether the result is usable without you rewriting it.
```
