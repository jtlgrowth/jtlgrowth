  2. Copy this line, right-click inside PowerShell to paste, press Enter. On a Mac, use the Mac line under the steps and paste with Cmd+V.

  3. When it asks for the Workshop key, type the key from the whiteboard.It keeps installing. This takes a few minutes.

Activity 3 · 5 minutes · on paper · now for your own desk

Write the brief for your one task

At the end: five lines that tell your employee exactly what to do.

WHY · GWENAI is only as good as your instructions. Vague brief, vague output. Five parts means it cannot guess what you meant.
  1. Goal: what done looks like, in one line."A weekly ads summary management can read in 2 minutes."
  2. Input: which file or folder it reads."Last week's exports in ads/."
  3. Output: the format and the filename."One table plus 3 moves, saved as ads/report.md."
  4. Check: how you will know it is right."Totals match the export."
  5. Never: what it must not do or guess."Never invent a number that is not in the file."
  6. Stuck? Paste this into Claude Code with your rough idea. 
    Here is my brief: [brief]
Rewrite it with five parts: Goal, Input, Output, Check, Never.
Ask me about anything missing before you start.
    It asks what is missing, then gives you a clean brief.

Data rule: no passport or ID numbers, card or bank details, salaries or passwords. Use "Client A" and sample data.
