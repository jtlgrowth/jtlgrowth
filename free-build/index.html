    No domain yet? We buy one for you, and it is included in the monthly. Already have one? We connect yours, and it stays yours. Same price either way.

  2. 3-month lock-in

    ₱96,000 or USD 1,500 in total, counted from the day your website goes live. After that, you decide: keep it month to month, or stop.

  3. Month 1 is how you apply

    You pay month 1 on this screen. No calls and no reservation fee. Owner to confirm: It counts as the first month of your 3-month lock-in, the month your website goes live.

    Owner to confirm: If your website is not live within 60 days of your payment for a reason on our side, we refund month 1 in full. Days we spend waiting on your logo, photos or answers do not count.

    Your website goes offline when maintenance stops. Your logo, photos, words, and any domain you bought stay yours. Moving the website to another host is a paid handover, and we quote it when you ask.

    Written permission to show your website as a case study, one before and after number that matters to you (inquiries, bookings or sales), and a 20-minute review with us at day 30.

    One edit to what is already there, about 30 minutes of work: text, prices, hours, a photo, a link. Unused changes do not carry over. New info for your chatbot is always included and does not count as a change. We reply within 1 business day and finish small changes within 2.

  8. Anything bigger

    Extra pages, an online shop, a booking system, SEO, more automations or bigger changes are add-ons. You get the add-ons menu with prices after you join, and we always quote first. Usage fees for messages, SMS or AI are billed at cost.
